Source: ICIP 2026 author kit, <https://2026.ieeeicip.org/author-kit/>.

This notice is the *only* copyright text ICIP asks authors for. It is prose and
carries no ISBN. The ISBN belongs to the proceedings volume and is assigned by
IEEE to the conference publication, never handed to authors — which is why the
paper source carries no `\copyrightnotice` and no publication-ID line. See the
comment at the top of `PBC_ICIP2026_CameraReady.tex`.

The MIT licence in this repository covers the reference implementation. It does
not cover the paper PDF, whose copyright was transferred to IEEE via the
electronic copyright form at final submission.
